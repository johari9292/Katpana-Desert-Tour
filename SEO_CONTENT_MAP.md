## Semantic coverage

Use terms when they improve the answer, not as a visible keyword list.

- Cold desert: Katpana Cold Desert, Skardu dunes, Katpana access, winter conditions, sunset timing, fragile desert surface, responsible vehicle use.
- Skardu packages: private itinerary, airport transfer, accommodation standard, group size, family pacing, custom quote, inclusions, exclusions, cancellation terms.
- Deosai: Sheosar Lake, plateau access, seasonal road, Sadpara side, 4x4 suitability, weather buffer, protected-area conduct.
- K2 and Baltoro: Askole, Baltoro Glacier, Concordia, acclimatization, permits, registered operator checks, guide and porter systems, expedition insurance, contingency days.
- Karakoram transport: road conditions, flight contingency, experienced mountain driver, night-driving risk, route status, buffer day.

## Page template requirements

Each indexable destination, tour or guide needs:

1. A unique H1 that states the page's real task.
2. A direct answer or route overview before promotional content.
3. Route-specific constraints, season caveats and who the option fits.
4. Links to its parent cluster, matching commercial page, safety page and booking terms.
5. A truthful conversion path: itemized quote request, not invented availability or price.
6. Literal image alt text; decorative images use empty alt text.
7. Only structured data supported by visible content and verified fields.

Generated prose never becomes indexable merely because it targets a keyword. Follow `CONTENT_EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md` and the separate travel-update approval workflow.

## Cannibalization review

Before approving a new URL, search the repository and Search Console for the proposed primary query. Update the canonical parent when the intent is substantially the same. Create a supporting page only when the user task, required evidence and conversion step are distinct. Redirect superseded pages to the closest useful parent and remove them from navigation and the sitemap.
