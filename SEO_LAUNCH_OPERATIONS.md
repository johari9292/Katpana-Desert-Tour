# SEO launch operations

These steps require production, analytics or business-profile access and therefore cannot be completed from the repository alone.

## Before launch

- Replace placeholder business gaps only with verified facts: legal/business name, physical address if customers can visit, operating hours, registration/licence details, accepted payments and genuine service prices.
- Confirm every published phone number and WhatsApp account is controlled by the business.
- Retain proof of ownership or permission for the project-supplied homepage hero and local video; replace either asset if that evidence is unavailable.
- Have a local route specialist approve the first indexable article and each freshness-led update.
- Have a qualified professional review booking, cancellation and privacy wording for the actual business process and applicable law.

## Deployment verification

1. Confirm the apex domain permanently redirects to `https://www.katpanadesert.com/` in one hop.
2. Test representative pages in Google's Rich Results Test and Schema.org Validator.
3. Inspect canonicals, `noindex` directives and the XML sitemap in the deployed HTML.
4. Submit `/sitemap.xml` to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
5. Request indexing for the homepage, travel-guide hub, tour hub, destination hub and strongest reviewed cluster parents.
6. Check the Coverage/Page Indexing report after recrawl; do not force-index editorial drafts.

## Measurement

- Connect a consent-compatible analytics destination to the existing conversion and `web_vital` events.
- Record WhatsApp clicks, inquiry starts, inquiry submits and custom-itinerary clicks by page/placement.
- Use Search Console query/page data to find high-impression low-CTR snippets and pages where two URLs compete for one intent.
- Review mobile Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile; follow `PERFORMANCE_BUDGET.md` for thresholds.

## Authority development

- Build a complete Google Business Profile only if the business meets Google's eligibility rules and can supply a verifiable real-world location or service-area setup.
- Seek relevant citations from official/local tourism bodies, accommodation partners, responsible travel organizations and expedition resources; avoid paid bulk links and irrelevant directories.
- Publish genuine trip feedback only with permission, source, trip context and date. Do not add self-serving review schema to business-controlled testimonials.
- Refresh permits, route access, security, transport and weather-sensitive content from primary sources and named local evidence.
